Exercises to try in the sandbox

Beginner

Menu detective

  • List every item in the menu table, cheapest first.
  • Find every item priced under $5.
  • Count how many items are in each category.
Intermediate

Customers & orders

  • Find every customer who has placed more than one order.
  • Compute each customer's total spend using SUM and GROUP BY.
  • List customers who have never placed an order (hint: LEFT JOIN).
Advanced

Push further

  • Segment customers into high / medium / low spenders with CASE.
  • Find the top-spending customer using a subquery, without hardcoding a number.
  • Write a CTE that ranks menu items by revenue if every order item were the same category.
