Semester Project: pick your industry, run its data

Working in teams of three or four, you'll act as an analytics consulting group reporting to a COO: design a database for a real business, query it to find operational bottlenecks, and ship the results as a live Streamlit dashboard. It's one project in two parts: a midterm design-and-build submission, then a final analysis with an individual oral defense.

R

Retail & E-Commerce

~100,000 real Olist orders across eight linked tables. Somewhere in there, a seller-region pair is quietly driving late deliveries and lost review stars.

D

Last-Mile Delivery & Logistics

Depots, drivers, vehicles, routes, and delivery attempts, modeled on three years in last-mile logistics. A failed first attempt costs more than a successful one; the data says exactly how much.

H

Hospitality & Marketplace

An Inside Airbnb city extract: listings, calendars, reviews. Which neighborhoods are supply-constrained, and what actually separates a high-occupancy host from an average one?

Teams may also propose their own dataset with instructor approval, including healthcare operations.

Generative AI tools are not permitted on the Semester Project, Parts 1 and 2. Tools like Grammarly for language help are fine. Every query you submit should be one you can defend out loud.
