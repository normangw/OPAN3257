OPAN 3257, Develop/Managing Data Bases. From your first query to a dashboard you can put in a portfolio.

SQL SQLite DuckDB Google Colab Streamlit No prior coding required

Why this course

A point-of-sale terminal, a shopping cart, a flight reservation, a bank balance to the cent: each one is really a database wearing a business costume. This course treats reading, questioning, and designing that database as a core management skill, not a specialist's trick. You'll build with SQLite, which runs the business day to day (OLTP), and query with DuckDB, a free warehouse-grade engine that studies the business the way analysts do at work (OLAP).

This course assumes you know nothing about databases or coding yet, and it doesn't stay that way for long: each class builds directly on the last. Two things carry you through the semester and outlast it — a multi-table business database you designed yourself, and a live Streamlit dashboard built on top of it that you can point people to after the class ends.

The arc of the semester

Seven verbs carry the course, in order. The full walk-through, with a runnable example for each, is on Topics.

1

Query

Ask precise questions of data with SELECT and WHERE.

2

Relate

Connect customers to orders to products with JOIN.

3

Design

Model a business process as tables, keys, and constraints.

4

Write

INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, and why the transaction is what keeps a ledger honest.

5

View

Package queries into reusable reports with views and CTEs.

6

Optimize

See what an index actually costs, and what it buys you.

7

Scale

From a file on your laptop to DuckDB, warehouses, and the cloud.
