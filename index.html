Why this course

Behind every consequential business application sits a database: the point-of-sale terminal, the cart holding your online order, the reservation system, the ledger that knows your bank balance to the cent. This course treats reading, questioning, and designing databases as a core management skill, not a specialist's trick. You'll work with SQLite to build and run a business database (OLTP), and DuckDB — a free, warehouse-grade analytical engine — to study it the way analysts do at work (OLAP).

This course assumes you know nothing about databases or coding. We start from zero and build step-by-step. By the end of the semester you'll have a functioning multi-table business database, a portfolio-ready analysis, and a live Streamlit dashboard at a URL you can share.

The arc of the semester

Seven verbs, each recast for business — see the Topics page for the full walk-through:

1. Query

Ask precise questions of data with SELECT and WHERE.

2. Relate

Connect customers to orders to products with JOIN.

3. Design

Model a business process as tables, keys, and constraints.

4. Write

INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE — and why transactions keep a ledger honest.

5. View

Package queries into reusable reports with views and CTEs.

6. Optimize

Why indexes make queries fast, and what that costs.

7. Scale

From a file on your laptop to DuckDB, warehouses, and the cloud.
